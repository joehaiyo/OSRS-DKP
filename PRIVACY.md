🛡️ Clan DKP Bot — Privacy PolicyThis Privacy Policy explains how our internal Discord bot collects, stores, and handles user data. Our priority is to keep your information safe while maintaining a transparent and secure gaming environment for our clan members.1. What Data We CollectOur bot interacts with the Discord API and collects only the minimal public information necessary to manage point balances and rank progressions:Discord User ID Handles: A unique numerical string (e.g., 123456789012345678) assigned by Discord to track individual account balances accurately.Discord Server Display Names: Your current nickname within our specific server, used to display readable names on logs, rosters, and leaderboards.Activity Milestones: Timestamps of when you registered for an event, checked into an active campaign, or spent point balances.2. How Your Data Is StoredDatabase: All point balances are written into a single data tracking spreadsheet file named dkp_data.json.Host Location: This data file is stored privately within our secure, cloud-synced GitHub repository (OSRS-DKP). It is not hosted on any open external website or third-party marketing server.Script Framework: The bot code runs within secure, isolated serverless container configurations hosted on Google Cloud Run.3. Data Retention and ErasureRetention: Your data is kept in the tracking system as long as you remain a member of our Discord server.Data Removal: If you leave the server, your account details will remain archived in the historical logs unless an Administrator manually purges your string profile.Right to Erase: You may request a complete erasure of your tracking records at any time by contacting a server Administrator. Your profile ID will be deleted from the database file, resetting your historical metrics to zero.4. Data Sharing and SecurityNo Third-Party Access: We do not sell, trade, or share your Discord identifiers with outside entities, advertisement networks, or commercial trackers.No Sensitive Information: The bot code contains no routines capable of reading your email addresses, real names, financial details, or Old School RuneScape account passwords.
